## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added

- 100 scenes across 10 places, built on eight puzzle types: balance levers, light and mirrors, shadows, water flow, parcel routing, combination locks, gear ratios and cargo packing.
- A daily scene, generated from the date.
- Explicit submit: nothing is judged until you submit, then the result plays out, with a ghost of your last attempt to compare against.
- Hints drawn from real life, and a reveal after each solve that names the maths behind it.
- A notebook of every concept you have met.
- A profile with a cosIQ score, ranks and a shareable card.
- Eight languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Assamese, Tamil, Telugu, Indonesian and Korean.
- Synthesized sound effects and haptics, with settings to turn each off and to reduce motion.
- Optional signed remote level packs.
- Progress saved in the platform's secure storage.

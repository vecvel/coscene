### Added

- 300 scenes in 30 topics, from easy to medium to hard: binary, bitwise operators, logic, logarithms, counting, modulo, binary search, two pointers, stacks, queues, bit tricks, sorting, hashing, recursion, sliding window, prefix sums, greedy choices, heaps, breadth-first search, combinatorics, dynamic programming, knapsack, shortest paths, topological sort, searching the answer, XOR tricks, union–find, probability, caching, and rate limiting with load balancing.
- Five puzzle types, each with a solver that proves every scene can be solved: answer, probe (a search where lucky guesses never help), pick, ops and route.
- A daily scene, generated from the date.
- Explicit submit, with a ghost of the last attempt.
- Hints from a nudge to the method, and a reveal that names the concept and where it comes up.
- A notebook of every concept found.
- A profile with a cosIQ score, ranks and a shareable card.
- Eight languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Assamese, Tamil, Telugu, Indonesian and Korean.
- Synthesized sound effects and haptics, with settings to turn each off and to reduce motion.
- Optional signed remote level packs.
- Progress saved in the platform's secure storage.
